# Code of Conduct

HarnessForge welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Treat others with respect, discuss ideas constructively, and accept feedback with care.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and welcoming to newcomers.
- Critique ideas and code respectfully; avoid personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone officially represents HarnessForge.

Report concerns privately to Vas Kuruganti at [vkuruganti1@gmail.com](mailto:vkuruganti1@gmail.com), using the subject `HarnessForge conduct report`. Include relevant context or links. Reports will be handled with care, with information shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove harmful content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant for serious or repeated violations. Responses should reflect the severity and impact of the conduct. Affected participants may request reconsideration privately through the reporting address.
